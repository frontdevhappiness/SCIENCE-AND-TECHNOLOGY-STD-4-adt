Chapter One

Health principles

Introduction

Good health is important for our well-being. In this chapter, you will learn about health principles. These include eating a balanced diet, doing physical exercises and maintaining both personal and environmental cleanliness. However, you will also explore safety signs and other important health information in the environment. The competencies developed will enable you to follow health principles. You will also be able to protect your health and the health of others.

Concept of health principles

Following health principles is important for staying healthy. Good health means being strong in both body and mind. To be healthy, you need to eat a balanced diet, do physical exercises regularly and ensure personal and environmental cleanliness.
