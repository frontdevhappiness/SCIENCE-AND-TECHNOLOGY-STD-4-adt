Balanced diet

A balanced diet is a meal with all the food nutrients in the correct proportions. It is made up of foods from three main groups. The groups are energy-giving foods, body-building foods, protective foods. Energy-giving foods have carbohydrate and fat nutrients. Body-building foods have protein nutrients. Protective foods have vitamins and minerals nutrients. Eating a balanced diet helps make the body’s immune system strong to fight off diseases.
