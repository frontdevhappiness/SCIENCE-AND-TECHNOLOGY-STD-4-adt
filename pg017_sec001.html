Activity 5

Use the library or reliable online sources to find information about different types of physical exercises. Then, do the exercises that you are able to perform.

Benefits of exercising

It is good to do physical exercises regularly. Regular physical exercises help:

(a) Stay healthy and strong;
(b) Keep body systems like digestion and excretory systems working well;
(c) Strengthen body immunity to fight different diseases;
(d) Reduce stress and depression; and
(e) Refresh the mind and have fun.

Effects of not doing exercises

Lack of physical exercises can cause problems like:

(a) Having poor health and weak body;
(b) Gaining too much weight, leading to obesity;
(c) Getting chronic diseases like high blood pressure, diabetes, and heart problems;
(d) Feeling stressed and depressed; and
(e) Having weak muscles and bones.

Personal and environmental hygiene

Maintaining hygiene of the body and the environment is important for good health. Body and environment must be kept clean all the time.
