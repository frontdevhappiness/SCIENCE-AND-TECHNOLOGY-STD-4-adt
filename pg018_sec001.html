Personal hygiene

Personal hygiene is important for our health. The body and clothes should be cleaned using proper tools to avoid diseases. Personal hygiene includes cleaning the mouth, skin, hair, nails and clothes.

Oral hygiene

The mouth should be cleaned using a toothbrush, toothpaste, and clean, safe water. Brushing removes food particles from the teeth. If the food particles remain, bacteria in the mouth feed on them and produce acid. This acid damages the teeth and causes cavities. It is important to brush your teeth at least twice a day. Brush your teeth in the morning when you wake up and at night before going to bed. This helps keep your teeth healthy.

Bathing

Clean water and soap should be used for bathing. It is good to bathe every day, in the morning and evening to avoid skin diseases. If we do not bathe, our bodies can smell bad. After bathing, dry the body with a clean towel. Apply body oil to keep the skin soft and prevent it from drying. We should also wash our faces with clean water and soap after waking up. This helps remove rheum and prevent eye diseases.
