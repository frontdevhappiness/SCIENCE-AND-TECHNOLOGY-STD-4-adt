Hair hygiene

Hair should be cared for by regularly washing with clean water and soap. It is also important to care the hair by braiding, shaving or combing to look clean and neat. Keeping hair clean helps prevent dandruff and pests like lice. This also helps prevent scalp infections such as ringworms.

Nail hygiene

Fingernails and toenails should be kept short and clean by cutting them regularly. Long nails can hold dirt with germs that cause diseases. Washing hands and nails with clean running water and soap helps remove these germs.

Clothing hygiene

Clothes should be washed with clean water and soap to remove dirt. Wearing dirty clothes can cause body odour and lead to skin problems like itching and rashes. After washing, clothes should be properly dried and ironed. Ironing helps kill germs and keeps the clothes looking neat.
