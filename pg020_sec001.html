Environmental hygiene

Dirty environments are harmful to our health. Dirty places attract insects like houseflies and mosquitoes that can spread diseases like diarrhoea, cholera and malaria. Breathing in polluted air can cause respiratory diseases. These diseases make our bodies weak. To stay healthy and strong, we must keep our surroundings clean.
