Vocabulary

Adolescent
someone who is no longer a child but is not yet an adult
Cell
smallest unit that makes living organism
Expiration
when a product is no longer safe to use because its time has run out
Nutrients
substances in foods that help our bodies work properly
Obesity
being overweight
Pathogens
living things that can cause diseases
Pesticides
chemicals used to kill or stop harmful bugs and plants that can harm crops and animals
Rheum
watery or sticky liquid that collects in the corners of the eyes, especially when sleeping
Scalp
skin covering the top of the head where hair grows
