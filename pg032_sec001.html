Concept of diseases

Disease is an abnormal condition or disorders in a living organism that disrupts normal functioning of the body. Many diseases are caused by germs found in our surroundings. These germs are found in air, soil, water, unsafe food and animal bodily fluids like blood. Germs can enter the body through eating contaminated food, drinking unsafe water, breathing in contaminated air, or touching an infected person. However, some diseases are not caused by germs, such as hereditary diseases.
