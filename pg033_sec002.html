Types of diseases

There are different types of diseases that affect the human body. These diseases are grouped into two main types namely: communicable diseases and non-communicable diseases.

Communicable diseases

Communicable diseases are illnesses that spread from one person to another. These diseases are caused by pathogens like bacteria and viruses. Examples of communicable diseases are malaria, cholera, chickenpox, tuberculosis, hepatitis and Corona Virus Disease of 2019 (COVID-19).

Malaria

Malaria is a disease caused by parasites called plasmodium. The disease is spread by a female mosquito called Anopheles.
