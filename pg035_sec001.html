Control and prevention of malaria

Malaria can be controlled by destroying places where mosquitoes breed. These places include bushes, long grass, and stagnant water in puddles. Tyres, coconut shells and cans that hold water can also serve as breeding areas for mosquitoes. Kerosene or larvicide can be spread on puddles to kill mosquito larvae. Also, coconut shells and other breeding areas should be destroyed. Malaria can also be prevented by sleeping under a treated mosquito net. Other ways include using mosquito sprays, coils and repellents. Also, putting wire mesh on windows and doors to prevent mosquitoes from entering the house. If someone has malaria symptoms, they should go to the hospital for diagnosis and treatment.
