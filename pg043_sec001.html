Symptoms of diabetes

Symptoms of diabetes include:

  • (a)Frequent urination;
  • (b)Rapid weight loss;
  • (c)Feeling thirsty even after drinking water;
  • (d)Feeling tired all the time;
  • (e)Poor eyesight vision;
  • (f)Wounds that heal slowly; and
  • (g)Numbness in hands or feet.

Prevention of diabetes

Diabetes can be prevented by living a healthy lifestyle. This includes eating properly and controlling body weight. Regular exercise can also lower the risk of diabetes.
