Symptoms of asthma

Symptoms of asthma include:

  • (a)Coughing, sneezing and a runny nose;
  • (b)Tightness and pain in the chest and ribs; and
  • (c)Shortness of breath with a wheezing or whistling sound.

Prevention of asthma

Asthma is a chronic condition with no cure. Asthma attacks can be prevented by:

  • (a)Avoiding asthma triggers like pollen and perfumes; and
  • (b)Getting treatment immediately after getting asthma symptoms.
