Activity 3: Investigating the development stages of a housefly

Materials: A hand lens/accessible application tools, a piece of raw meat, plastic wrap, pins and a plate

Procedure

  1. 1.Place the piece of meat on the plate.
  2. 2.Place the plate with the meat in an open space where houseflies can land on it.
  3. 3.Use a hand lens/accessible application tools to look/observe at the meat on the plate. Check for any living organisms. What do you observe?
  4. 4.After 24 hours, observe how the eggs have changed into larvae.
  5. 5.Continue to observe periodically how larvae change into pupae by answering the following questions:
    • (a)What kind of shield or casing covers these creatures?
    • (b)After how many days did these creatures change?
    • (c)Are these creatures moving?
  6. 6.Count ten pupae and leave them on the plate.
  7. 7.Cover the plate with a plastic wrap. Make small holes in the plastic wrap with a pin.
  8. 8.Continue observing the pupae and write down your observations guided by the following questions:
    • (a)How many pupae are left on the plate?
    • (b)What happened to the other pupae?
    • (c)What other organisms can you see/observe on the plate?
