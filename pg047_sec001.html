Activity 4: Investigating the stages of mosquito development

Materials: Hand lens/accessible application tools, a bottle or coconut shell, and a fine mesh net

Procedure

  1. 1.Collect water from a stagnant pool that has been there for a long time. Make sure the water has mosquito eggs or larvae.
  2. 2.Place some mosquito eggs or larvae from the water into a bottle or coconut shell.
  3. 3.After two days use a hand lens to observe water for changes of eggs into larvae.
  4. 4.Cover the bottle or coconut shell containing water with larvae with a fine mesh net. Make sure it is securely closed.

Questions

(a) What did you observe during your investigation?
(b) Draw a diagram or explain the development stage of a mosquito.
