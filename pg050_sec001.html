Vocabulary

Antiseptic
a chemical used to kill germs in clothes and other areas
Allergy
a condition where the body reacts to things like dust, pollen, or certain smells that do not harm others
Blisters
fluid-filled bumps that appear on the skin
Germs
very small living organisms that cause diseases and cannot be seen with the naked eye
Pathogen
harmful microorganisms such as bacteria, virus, fungus or parasite that causes diseases
Transfusion
to receive blood from another person usually in the hospital
Tumour
swelling of a part of the body, generally without inflammation, caused by an abnormal growth of cell
