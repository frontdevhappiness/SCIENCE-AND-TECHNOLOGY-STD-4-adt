Chapter Three

Matter

Introduction

The environment comprises substances that occupy space and have mass. Such substances are called matter. In this chapter, you will learn about the states of matter. You will also conduct simple experiments to investigate their properties. The competencies developed will enable you to use various types of matter in everyday life.

Think Various things that have mass and occupy space

Concept of matter

Matter is anything that has mass and occupies space. Substances like rocks, water and air are examples of matter.

Activity 1

1.Use library or reliable online sources to find information on the concept of matter, then provide other examples of matter.
2.Collect various matter samples, observe their properties, and categorise them into the three states of matter: solid, liquid, and gas.
