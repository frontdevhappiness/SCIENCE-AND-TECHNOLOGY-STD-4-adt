Investigating the properties of matter

Matter can change from one state to another depending on changes in temperature. The following experiments 1-4, will demonstrate the three states of matter using water.

Experiment 1: Investigating the properties of water in the solid state
Aim:
To demonstrate the properties of water in the solid state
Materials:
Six equal-sized ice cubes, a saucepan, an ice box, a flat surface such as tray, a jug, a heat source (such as a stove) and accessible application tools
Note:
Ensure the ice is from safe and clean water.
