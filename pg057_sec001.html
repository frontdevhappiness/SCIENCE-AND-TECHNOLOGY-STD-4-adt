Experiment 4: Changing gas to a liquid state

Aim:
To demonstrate changing of gas to liquid state
Materials:
Mirror or glass, water, a kettle, a heat source (stove) and accessible application tools

Procedure

1.

Pour water into the kettle.

2.

3.
