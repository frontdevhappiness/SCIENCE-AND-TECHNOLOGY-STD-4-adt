When this gas comes into contact with a cooler object like glass or mirror, it condenses and appears as mist. This mist continues to cool and turns into a liquid state. This demonstrates that when gas is cooled, it changes into a liquid. The process is called condensation. Therefore, condensation is the process of changing gas to liquid state.

Freezing point of water

Water freezes when it reaches a specific temperature. The freezing point of water is the exact temperature at which a liquid (water) freezes and changes into a solid (ice). Ice has many uses, such as preserving food and beverages. The coldness helps slow bacterial growth and prevent spoilage of foods.
