Procedure

1.

Place a plastic cup filled with water on the table.

2.

Take the thermometer and immerse it in the water, holding it from above as shown in Figure 5.

3.

Read the temperature. Make sure you get help reading the water temperature if needed. Record the temperature.

4.

Place the cup of water in the freezer. Measure the temperature of the water every 10 minutes. Continue measuring the temperature until ice starts to form. Record the observations.

5.

Compare the temperature of the water in its solid state to its liquid state.
