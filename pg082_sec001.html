Experiment 3: Investigating transfer of heat in liquids

Aim:
To demonstrate transfer of heat in liquids
Materials:
A cooking pot/accessible cooking pot, a stove, a matchstick, water, small pieces of A4 paper, a thermometer/audio thermometer and accessible application tools
Caution:
Exercise caution throughout the experiment to avoid fire hazards.

Procedure

1.
Take small pieces of paper and put them in the cooking pot/accessible cooking pot.
2.
Add cold water into the cooking pot/accessible cooking pot and leave it for five minutes as shown in Figure 3(a). What happens to the small pieces of paper?
3.
Measure the temperature of the water using a thermometer/audio thermometer and record it.
4.
Place the pot with the pieces of paper in water on the lit stove as shown in Figure 3(b).
5.
Wait for ten minutes, then measure the water temperature using a thermometer/audio thermometer and record it.
6.
What happened to the pieces of paper and the water temperature?

Write the results as in Table 1.
