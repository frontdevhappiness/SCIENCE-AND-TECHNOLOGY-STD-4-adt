Uses of heat energy

Heat energy can be used for various purposes as shown in Table 2.

Table 2: Uses of heat energy

PictureExplanation
Using heat to stay warm against the cold.
Ironing clothes to kill germs and make them look neat.
Drying crops to remove moisture that could cause spoilage.

Other uses of heat include; cooking food, boiling drinking water, melting metals in industries and generating electricity. Additionally, heat is used in the production of various products such as metals, plastics and glasses.
