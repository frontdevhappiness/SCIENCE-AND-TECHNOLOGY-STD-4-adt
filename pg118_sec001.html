After drawing the shapes and saving your work, the next step is to colour shapes.

Colouring shapes

To colour the shapes, use the following steps.

Steps

1. Click/press on the colour ‘Red’ as shown in Figure 10.

2. Click/press on ‘Fill’.

3. Click/press inside the right-angled triangle and apply the ‘Red’ colour. The triangle will be in red as shown in Figure 11.
