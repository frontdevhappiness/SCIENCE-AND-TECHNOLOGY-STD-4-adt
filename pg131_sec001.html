Steps

1.
Click/press on the ‘Events’ block menu, drag/use arrow keys and drop the ‘when clicked/pressed’ block on the script area.
2.
Click/press on the ‘Motion’ block menu, drag/use arrow keys and drop the ‘go to (random position)’ block on the script area.
3.
Attach the ‘go to (random position)’ block to the ‘when clicked/pressed’ block.
4.
Drag/use arrow keys and drop the ‘turn (↺) 15 degrees’ block on the script area.
5.
Attach the ‘turn (↺) 15 degrees’ block to the ‘go to (random position)’ block.
6.
Change the degrees from 15 to 30 degrees.
7.
Drag/use arrow keys and drop the ‘if on edge, bounce’ block on the script area.
8.
Attach the ‘if on edge, bounce’ block to the “turn () degrees” block.”
