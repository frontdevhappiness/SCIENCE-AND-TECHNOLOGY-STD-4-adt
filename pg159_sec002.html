Section C: Practical questions

10. Prepare a program that can play a sound and move the Sprite 35 steps.
11. Prepare a program that allows the Sprite to play a sound and move 15 steps, then have the program repeat 12 times.
12. (a) Prepare a program that enables the Sprite to go to any position using the “greater than” operator. Enter the number 8 in the first box and 5 in the second box. Then, try running your program. What happens after you try it?
(b) Repeat question 12(a) by entering the number 9 in the first box and 13 in the second box. What happens?
13. Draw a rectangle and place it on top of two circles of equal size. One circle should be on the left side and the other on the right side. Colour the drawings.
